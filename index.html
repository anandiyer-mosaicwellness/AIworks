Counter-Strategy — Point by Point

How Man Matters should respond to each Traya creative angle

01

Hair Stages / Fear Framework

Their highest-impression creative angle
🚨 Their Angle
"Stage 5 = irreversible baldness. Act now or lose your hair forever." Urgency + fear framework that routes straight to purchase or quiz.
✅ Our Counter
Reframe: Stages don't matter if you can't follow through. "Starting is easy. Staying consistent is what actually grows your hair." Shift from fear → consistency + simplicity.
Actions 1Build 1 anchor creative around the consistency message 2Target men who tried treatments but dropped off 3Tagline: "Hair growth that fits your life. Not the other way around."
02

Regional Language Domination

Telugu + Tamil — highest impression volume
🚨 Their Angle
Telugu + Tamil creatives running since Dec 2025 — now their highest-impression ads. Same "93% success" script localised for South India.
✅ Our Counter
South Indian men already have longer grooming routines. A 7-product medicated kit on top is the last thing they want. "Your hair routine. 2 minutes. That's it."
Actions 1Launch Telugu first — 2-minute routine video format 2Use micro-influencers (100k–500k) in Hyd / Chennai / Bengaluru 3Follow with Tamil using same winning format
03

Copy-Paste Influencer Strategy

8+ faces, identical script
🚨 Their Angle
Same 4-line script given to all creators. MridulMadhok runs 5 versions of identical creative. Volume play over authenticity.
✅ Our Counter
When 8 faces say the same words, audiences recognise it as paid. Authenticity is the counter — unscripted UGC beats polished scripts. Real switchers > paid influencers.
Actions 1Brief: "Show your actual morning. Don't read anything. Under 2 mins." 2Source 5–6 genuine Traya switchers for UGC (P0 priority) 3Raw phone footage, bathroom lighting = signals real
04

Direct Minoxidil Attack

"3x more effective than Minoxidil" — in their top-impression ads
🚨 Their Angle
"3x more effective than Minoxidil" — explicitly named in highest-impression Telugu + Tamil ads. Direct attack on Man Matters' core product.
✅ Our Counter
Don't retreat — own it. Minoxidil is the most clinically proven hair treatment globally. The problem is using it alone. MM's stack (DHT blocker + biotin + scalp health) is the amplifier. "Minoxidil works. Here's what makes it work even better."
Actions 1Messaging: "Minoxidil works. Here's what makes it work even better." 2Show MM's DHT blocker + biotin + scalp health as the amplifier stack 3Lead with dermatologist endorsements if available
05

Quiz / App Funnel

Personalisation-first acquisition model
🚨 Their Angle
All traffic routed through form.traya.health hair test. App-first acquisition model with personalised plan as the hook.
✅ Our Counter
Build a faster quiz — 3 questions, 60 seconds, ends with a specific named product recommendation. Personalisation feeling > actual depth. Speed and confidence beat complexity.
Actions 1Max 3–4 questions: concern, duration, previous treatment 2End with confident named recommendation + reason why 3Test quiz flow vs direct PDP — expect 20–35% CVR lift

Kit Complexity — Man Matters' Biggest Opportunity

Real user feedback: Traya's kit is too complex to follow daily

📌 Core Insight

Real Traya users say the kit is too complex to maintain daily. Compliance over 6 months determines hair regrowth — not which product is used. A simpler routine followed every day beats a superior one abandoned in month 2.
